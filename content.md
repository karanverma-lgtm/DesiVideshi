## About

### Hero

### Our Story
For years, Indian catering meant picking a lane — either purely traditional, or "fusion" in name only. We built Desi Videshi Chaat to actually live up to its name: street food rooted in Indian tradition, served alongside genuine global twists, all cooked live in front of your guests.

We don't believe in standing buffets where food goes cold under a heat lamp. We believe in counters — chefs working the pan in real time, guests lining up because they want to, not because they have to.

Every menu we build is custom. We don't hand you a fixed card and ask you to pick from it; we ask what your event needs, and we build the counters around that.

### What Makes Us Different (4 pillars)
1. **Authenticity First** — Every fusion dish starts from a real, traditional base — we're not skipping the original to chase a trend.
2. **Genuine Innovation** — Our "videshi" twists are built by chefs who've actually cooked both cuisines, not a menu gimmick.
3. **Presentation That Performs** — Live counters, plating, and setup designed to be part of the event itself.
4. **Service End to End** — Setup, service, and cleanup — you focus on your guests, we handle the rest.
